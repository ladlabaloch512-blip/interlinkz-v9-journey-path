What a Business Website Should Include

Interlinkz Blog · 14 November 2026

A home page that explains you fast

Your home page should say what your business does in the first few seconds. Use a clear headline, a short line about who you help, and a button that leads to the next step. Visitors decide quickly, so keep the top of the page simple and direct.

Below that, show your main services, a short introduction to your business and a clear way to contact you. If you have examples of your work or demo pages, link to them so visitors can see more.

Service pages with real detail

Each main service deserves its own page. Explain what the service includes, who it is for and how a customer starts. Clear service pages also help you appear in search results, because people search for the service, not only for your business name.

Keep the words simple. Write the way you would explain the service to a customer standing in front of you.

A business website has one main job: help a visitor understand what you do and reach you in seconds.

An about page that builds trust

People buy from businesses they trust. Your about page should say who you are, what you do and how you work. You do not need a long story. A short, honest page with your way of working is enough.

If your process has clear steps, show them. Visitors relax when they can see what happens after they contact you.

Contact details that are easy to find

Every page should make contact easy. Show your email address clearly and include a contact form for people who prefer writing a message. Check the form yourself from a phone before the site goes live, and make sure the message reaches an inbox that a person reads every day.

A website that hides its contact details loses customers quietly. Make reaching you the easiest action on the page.

The short checklist

  • A home page that explains your business fast
  • Service pages with real detail
  • An about page that builds trust
  • Contact details that are easy to find
